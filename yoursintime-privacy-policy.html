Yours in Time · Prizm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Yours in Time stores your planning entries on your device. Prizm does not operate a backend that receives those entries. No app account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or AI service is used.

1. Scope and provider

This policy explains how Prizm handles information in Yours in Time, displayed on your device as Your Time (the “App”), and when you contact us. The App helps you visualize time assets, important people, and life chapters using assumptions you choose. It does not make lifespan or health predictions.

2. Information you enter

The App processes the following on your device so that its features work:

  • Your date of birth and chosen planning age or time horizon.
  • Names or nicknames, relationship labels, optional birth years, meeting labels, meeting frequencies, and horizons you enter for important people.
  • Life chapter titles and years, custom asset titles and frequencies, selected icons or colors, and app preferences.
  • The widget item you select and a local snapshot of values and labels used by the widget extension.

These entries are stored locally using iOS app storage, including UserDefaults. The App does not upload them to Prizm. We do not require an App login or import information from your address book. You can use nicknames and omit optional personal details.

3. Widgets and visible information

The App and its widget extension share a local App Group container so widgets can read your selected metrics, labels, and relevant planning inputs. This is local sharing between components of this App, not sharing with unrelated apps or a Prizm server.

Depending on your widget selection and device settings, names, chapter titles, and counts can appear on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Anyone who can see that screen may see the displayed information. Choose non-sensitive labels, change the selection, or remove the widget if needed.

4. Purchases and Apple services

Optional in-app purchases and restoration use Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes your payment, account, and transaction information under its own policies. The App reads product information and verified transaction entitlements to determine whether paid features are unlocked. Prizm does not receive your payment-card details or your Apple Account password. App Store sales or reporting information may be made available to us by Apple; we do not combine it with your local planning entries.

The App does not use RevenueCat or another third-party purchase-management SDK. Learn more about Apple's privacy practices. Product loading, purchase verification, and restoration may use the internet even though core planning works offline.

5. Backups and diagnostics

“Local-first” does not mean that your device can never back up app data. Depending on your settings, Apple's iCloud Backup, computer backups, or device migration may include or restore app data. These processes are managed by you and Apple, not by a Prizm cloud-sync service. See what iCloud backs up and manage backups in your Apple settings.

We do not integrate an analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting SDK. Apple may process or make available diagnostics or aggregated App Store statistics according to its services and your device privacy settings. We do not use these to track your planning entries, and we do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

6. Permissions

The current App does not request access to your contacts, calendars, camera, microphone, photo library, or precise location, and does not request push-notification or App Tracking Transparency permission. Adding a widget is controlled by iOS and does not grant the App access to those resources.

7. Email support and this website

If you email us, we receive the address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to support requests and meet applicable legal obligations. Avoid sending private information about other people or sensitive screenshots unless necessary. Support messages are handled through our email provider and retained only as needed for support and applicable legal requirements.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends ordinary web-request information to the hosting provider; this is separate from using the App offline. This page has no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, or third-party fonts. Hosting is subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

8. Retention and deletion

Your local entries remain until you edit or delete them, reset app data in Settings, or remove the App's local data. Resetting data replaces the local widget snapshot; an already displayed widget may continue to show cached content until iOS refreshes it. Remove widgets if you need to hide their content immediately.

Deleting the App or resetting its entries does not delete Apple's purchase records, separately retained backups, or support messages. You control backups through Apple or your computer. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to legal retention requirements. Because we do not receive your planning entries, we cannot retrieve or delete those entries remotely for you.

9. Your rights and information about others

You can inspect, change, and delete information in the App. Depending on your location, applicable privacy laws may also give you access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or complaint rights concerning personal information we handle, such as support emails. Contact us to exercise these rights. Local-only processing does not remove protections that may apply under the GDPR, Korean privacy law, or other applicable law.

If you add information about a family member or child, enter only what is appropriate and respect their privacy. The App does not offer a public posting service or operate children's accounts. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through support; contact us if you believe such information has been sent to us inappropriately.

10. International processing

The App does not transfer your local planning entries to a Prizm backend. Apple, GitHub, and our email provider may process information involved in their respective services in other countries under their own arrangements and applicable law. Do not interpret on-device planning as a promise that every external service operates only in your country.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the App or our practices change. The current version and its effective date will be published here. Additional notice or consent will be provided when required by law.

For privacy questions or support-data requests, contact Prizm at prizm_kr@hotmail.com.
