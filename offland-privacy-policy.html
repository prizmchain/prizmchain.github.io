OFFLAND · Prizm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Your Screen Time, focus history and world progress stay on your iPhone. OFFLAND does not require an account and does not upload those records. Purchase information is processed separately by Apple and RevenueCat; “local-first” does not mean that no data ever reaches a server.

1. Who this policy covers

Prizm (“we,” “us”) provides OFFLAND, an iPhone app that turns eligible time away from your phone into progress in a virtual world. This policy describes information processed by the App, its widgets and DeviceActivity extension, its purchase services, support communications and these public policy pages. Contact us at prizm_kr@hotmail.com.

2. Activity information stays local

When you authorize Apple's Screen Time access, OFFLAND uses system-provided activity to calculate aggregate device-use minutes, eligible offline periods and comparisons against a personal baseline. Local records can include daily summaries, goals, active hours, focus-session timing, energy, world stages and objects, achievements, preferences and a cached Premium status.

OFFLAND processes and stores these records on your device using Apple's persistence and protected-file facilities. Its own extensions share only the summaries and settings needed for their functions through an App Group. Selected-app tokens for optional shielding remain in this local authorization boundary; they are not sent to us or RevenueCat. Widget summaries do not contain a list of the apps you use.

We do not operate a server that receives your Screen Time, app selections, focus history, world records or achievement history. There is no OFFLAND account, CloudKit sync, advertising SDK, behavioral analytics SDK or AI service. We do not sell your personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. The App does not request contacts, camera, microphone, photo-library or location access.

3. Apple and RevenueCat purchase processing

When commerce is configured, the RevenueCat SDK can contact its service to load offers, verify or restore purchases and maintain Premium access. This is not limited to the moment you press Buy. Apple processes payment and handles your Apple Account and billing details under its own policies. OFFLAND does not receive your full Apple Account credentials or card details.

RevenueCat processes purchase history and entitlement information, including product and transaction identifiers, receipts or purchase-verification information, purchase/expiration dates and subscription status, associated with an SDK-generated anonymous App User ID. Standard service metadata can include app/SDK/OS versions, device type, locale, currency, request timing, and network information such as an IP address received by the service. An anonymous SDK ID is a pseudonymous commerce identifier, not a guarantee that transaction data is legally anonymous.

OFFLAND does not supply a custom personal user ID, email, name, advertising ID, attribution identifiers or customer attributes to RevenueCat. Automatic device-identifier collection and SDK diagnostics are disabled in our configuration. No Screen Time, selected-app tokens, focus records, goals or world data are attached to purchase requests.

Purchase information supports verification, fraud prevention, paid access, restoration and purchase/subscription reporting in RevenueCat's dashboard. Purchase reporting is separate from behavioral tracking of your activity in OFFLAND. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy for their processing practices.

4. Permissions and your choices

  • Screen Time / Family Controls: requested only after an explanation and your explicit action, for local activity summaries and optional selected-app shielding. You can revoke access in iOS Settings. Without it, the manual-baseline path and basic focus remain available; unavailable activity is not invented.
  • Notifications: optional, on-device reminders scheduled only after you opt in. You can change categories in OFFLAND or disable authorization in iOS Settings. We do not run a remote-push service.
  • Widgets: added by you. They show limited local world/progress summaries; consider your device's lock-screen privacy settings.

5. Support and public web pages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, for answering the request and keeping necessary support records. Please do not send Screen Time screenshots or sensitive app selections unless you choose to share them for a specific support issue. We do not receive that information automatically from OFFLAND.

These legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and network providers may process IP addresses and standard request logs when you open a page. We add no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, tracking cookies or external fonts to these OFFLAND legal pages. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. Opening external links is subject to the relevant site's privacy practices.

6. Retention and deletion

Local activity, focus and world records remain until you use OFFLAND's confirmed local-data deletion or remove the App's data. Deletion also clears its cached entitlement, shared summaries, shields and pending local notifications. Apple-managed backups, if you enable them, follow your device/Apple Account settings; OFFLAND provides no separate cloud backup or cross-device activity sync.

Local deletion or uninstalling does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat. Those records may be retained as needed for entitlement restoration, fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations under their applicable retention policies. We do not promise a fixed deletion period for records controlled by those services. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations.

To request access, correction or deletion of commerce/support information handled on our behalf, contact prizm_kr@hotmail.com. We may need limited purchase-reference information to locate a pseudonymous record and verify your request. Do not send card details or passwords. Deleting a RevenueCat customer record is separate from cancellation and may not remove legally retained Apple records; restoring a purchase can create or repopulate an entitlement record.

7. Purposes, rights and international processing

Depending on applicable law, the bases for processing include providing the App and purchases you request, your permission or consent where required, compliance with legal obligations, and legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention and responding to support. Optional OS authorization is not a blanket consent to upload your activity.

Apple, RevenueCat, GitHub and email providers can process commerce, web-request or support information outside your country under their service arrangements and applicable safeguards. Your local Screen Time and world records are not uploaded by OFFLAND for those purposes.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. These rights may be subject to identity verification and legal exceptions. For locally held activity records, use the App's controls; for other requests, contact us. We do not claim that local processing removes all privacy-law obligations.

8. Children and security

OFFLAND is not specifically directed to children. App Store content ratings can vary by region and OS version and are not a legal consent age. Where parental consent is legally required, a parent or guardian should supervise use and purchases. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without required consent, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

We minimize shared data, use Apple's device protection facilities and HTTPS for purchase-service requests. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. Protect access to your device and Apple Account; local world progress may not be recoverable if local data is lost.

9. Policy updates

We will update this page and its date when our practices change, with additional notice or consent where required. A change notice does not substitute for legally required permission. Questions or privacy requests: prizm_kr@hotmail.com.
