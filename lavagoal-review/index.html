A quick route through the distinctive mechanic

  1. Choose English → My court → Free play. Practice setup has no time limit.
  2. Keep Middle selected. Compare High bounce and Low bounce, using Bounce again to replay the physical trial.
  3. Try Defend and Attack, then Ready. Drive, jump and boost on the chosen court.
  4. Open Court challenges → High hoop → Try driving for a short playable application of the panel mechanic.

Play and progression

Three court challenges are available from the garage. Solo cups contains nine CPU rivals across three cups, with stage unlocks, earned colours and patterns, and Champion rematches after all nine wins.

The paid download includes the complete game. Progression locks are earned by playing. No login, advertising, IAP or subscription is required.

Optional local 1v1

Use two iPhones with the same version on the same Wi-Fi. Allow Local Network access. One selects Play together → Create room; the other selects Join a room and the displayed LAVA room.

Bonjour discovers the host; a direct local TCP connection carries play. There is no internet matchmaking or developer account. The demonstration here covers one device; multiplayer instructions are provided in the PDF.
