PRIZM / Duo Prism

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Published by PRIZM_KR

Offline gameplay, local progress.
Duo Prism requires no developer account and contains no ads, tracking or behavioral analytics. Puzzle progress and preferences stay on your device. If you choose optional online records, Game Center handles player identity, eligible scores and achievements through Apple services.

1. Scope and publisher

This policy explains how Duo Prism (the “App”), its support services and these policy pages handle information. The publisher is PRIZM_KR. Contact prizm.dev.kr@gmail.com with privacy questions.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Puzzle progress: component placements, solved or skipped puzzles, hint stages and saved scenes.
  • Local game records: challenge runs, scores, solution history, earned achievements and laboratory designs.
  • Preferences: input mode, fold assistance, comfortable virtual angle range, sound, haptics, visual accessibility and optional online-record settings.
  • Pending online records: when you enable online records, the App may keep an Apple Game Center player identifier, eligible scores, achievement evidence and submission receipts locally so records can be associated with the correct player.

This data is used to operate the game, remember your choices, resume play and validate eligible online records. It is not uploaded to developer-operated servers. Duo Prism does not provide account-based cloud synchronization. Apple’s device backups may include local app data according to your system settings.

3. Fold input and device permissions

On supported hardware and OS versions, the App reads native fold input to adjust the virtual puzzle angle. Touch input provides the same type of virtual control on other supported devices. Current input and selected solution-angle or stability information may be used or retained locally as game state or solution evidence; we do not transmit raw fold-input streams to our servers. Required gameplay does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, photo-library or advertising-tracking access.

4. Optional Apple Game Center

Campaign puzzles, challenges and hints work without Game Center. If you enable optional online records and sign in, Apple supplies a game-specific player identifier. The App uses Apple’s GameKit services to submit eligible scores and earned achievements and display rankings or achievements. Apple processes this information under its own policies; your nickname, avatar and scores may be visible to other Game Center users, depending on Apple’s features and sharing settings.

You can play offline without enabling online records. Manage Game Center activity sharing in your Apple settings. Deleting local progress does not delete scores or achievements already held by Apple. Apple’s Reset Identifier setting changes its activity identifier; it does not erase scores or achievements. Read Game Center & Privacy for Apple’s processing and controls.

5. Information we do not collect through the App

The App has no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not operate a gameplay, account, chat or ranking server for Duo Prism. Local puzzle progress, touch gestures and device fold readings are not sent to us as analytics. We do not receive your Apple Account password or full payment-card details.

6. Support correspondence

If you voluntarily email prizm.dev.kr@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as app version, device model, puzzle name or an optional screenshot. We use it to respond and investigate the issue, and retain it only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. Do not send passwords, account identifiers, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of support information, subject to applicable law and retention requirements.

7. Apple services and this website

Apple handles App Store downloads and payments, optional Game Center services and any device backups under its own policies. Apple may provide developers with aggregate App Store reports or system diagnostics according to Apple’s settings and policies; these are not an analytics SDK embedded in Duo Prism.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and may process technical request information to serve the site. The pages we publish include no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking code. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising or share local saves with data brokers. Support correspondence is handled through our email provider, and information may be disclosed where necessary to comply with applicable law or protect legal rights. Apple services, support email and GitHub Pages hosting may process information in countries where those providers operate, under their policies and safeguards. Local gameplay information is not sent to developer-operated servers.

9. Security and retention

Local data uses the protections provided by the operating system’s app sandbox. No system can be guaranteed completely secure. Game data remains until replaced or removed. Deleting the App normally removes its local data; offloading is different from deletion, and system backups may retain copies. Apple controls the retention of records held by Game Center. Support correspondence is retained only for the support purpose or applicable legal requirements described above.

10. Your choices

  • Play the campaign, challenges and hints offline without enabling online records.
  • Change input, sound, haptic and accessibility preferences in the App.
  • Manage Game Center sign-in and activity sharing through Apple’s settings.
  • Use the operating system’s Delete App option, rather than Offload App, to remove local app data.
  • Manage device backups using Apple’s settings.
  • Contact us about support information that we control and any rights available under applicable law.

11. Children

Duo Prism does not require a developer account or request a child’s name, age or contact information for gameplay. Apple manages any child-account restrictions for Game Center. Parents and guardians should supervise optional online features and support email. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address the request and applicable deletion requirements.

12. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App or our practices. The date above identifies the current version. Material changes will receive notice or consent as required by applicable law.

13. Contact

Privacy questions and support: prizm.dev.kr@gmail.com.
