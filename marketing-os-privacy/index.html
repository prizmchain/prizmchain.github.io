Privacy Policy

PRIZM Marketing OS is an owner-operated desktop workflow used to prepare and distribute marketing content for PRIZM's independent apps. This policy explains how the workflow handles information when a social account owner authorizes it.

Information processed

The workflow may process account identifiers, profile information made available by an authorized platform, access and refresh tokens, videos and captions selected for publication, publishing status, post identifiers, and performance or engagement data returned by the platform.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the account owner, prepare or upload approved content, monitor publishing status, measure results, and improve PRIZM's marketing workflow. PRIZM does not sell personal information or use authorized platform data for unrelated advertising.

Storage and security

Credentials are stored locally on the owner's Mac in the encrypted VaultX application. Operational files and publishing records are kept locally or in PRIZM-controlled storage. Reasonable technical measures are used to restrict access to the owner-operated workflow.

Third-party services

When an account is connected, information is also processed by the applicable platform, including TikTok, under that platform's own terms and privacy policy. Apple, hosting providers, and media-storage providers may process limited technical data needed to operate PRIZM's apps and website.

Retention and deletion

Credentials are retained until they expire, are replaced, or the account owner disconnects the integration. Local campaign records are retained only as long as useful for operating and evaluating campaigns. To request deletion, revoke PRIZM Marketing OS in the connected platform and contact us at the address below.

Your choices

You may decline authorization, limit permissions when the platform offers that choice, revoke access through platform settings, or request deletion of locally retained information.
