Duo Fight support

Duo Fight brings disc football, mini curling, air hockey and disc knockout to one shared screen. Play locally with another person, practice against the computer, or try skill challenges.

Need help?
Email prizm.dev.kr@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, the game involved and the steps that caused the problem. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending them. Never send passwords or payment-card details.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is required. Gameplay works offline on the same device; Duo Fight does not offer online multiplayer.

How do two players start?

Choose a game and select side-by-side or face-to-face seating. Each player presses their own readiness button. Use a sufficiently wide playing area for face-to-face games; on a foldable device, fully open the screen before starting.

Can I play on my own?

Choose solo practice against the computer or a skill challenge from the lobby.

Where are names and game progress stored?

Player labels, preferences, challenge progress and resumable match data are stored locally. There is no account-based cloud sync. Deleting the app can remove its local progress; offloading the app is different from deleting it. Apple device backups may include app data according to your system settings.

Does the app contain ads or in-app purchases?

The current version has no advertising, subscriptions or in-app purchases. The App Store listing shows any download price that applies.
