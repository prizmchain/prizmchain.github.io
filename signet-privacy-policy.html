Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Summary: Signet. does not require an account, display ads, or track you across apps. Your signatures and imported documents are processed on your device. RevenueCat processes limited purchase information so paid access can be verified and restored.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or upload the names you enter, signature paths, imported document contents, exported files, contact information, location, advertising identifiers, browsing history, or behavioral analytics.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Signature data: Generated names, vector paths, and your selected reusable signature.
  • Local settings: App preferences, locally mirrored purchase access, and completed-export diagnostics.
  • Temporary files: Imported PDFs or images, PNG/SVG exports, and flattened signed documents created at your request.

Temporary working files are removed after success, cancellation, or failure. Imported document content is not retained by Signet. Generated signature data remains until it is replaced, deleted in the App, or removed with the App.

3. Purchase Information

Apple App StorePayment and purchase historyHandled by Apple under your Apple ID; we do not receive full payment-card details.
RevenueCatVerify and restore Unlimited Documents accessAnonymous app customer identifier, product and entitlement status, and purchase history. This information is not used for advertising or tracking.

4. How Information Is Used

Local information is used only to create and display signatures, pass a selected signature to the iOS Action Extension, place it on a document, produce user-requested exports, remember local settings, and enforce or restore paid access.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share signature or document content with advertising, analytics, or data-broker services. Limited purchase information is processed by Apple and RevenueCat solely for purchase functionality.

6. Security and Retention

Local files use the protections provided by the iOS app sandbox and file-protection system. No system is guaranteed to be completely secure. Purchase records may remain with Apple and RevenueCat as required for transaction history, fraud prevention, and purchase restoration.

7. Your Choices

  • Delete generated signature data from within the App.
  • Delete exported files from the location where you saved them.
  • Delete the App to remove its remaining local app data.
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests concerning information we control.

8. Children

Signet. is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required by local law, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International Processing

Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase information in countries where they operate, subject to their safeguards and privacy policies.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will identify the current version.
