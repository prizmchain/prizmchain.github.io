01 · Overview

A private, offline-first sound space.

This Privacy Policy explains how PRIZM ("PRIZM," "we," or "us"), the operator of Muryn, handles information when you use the Muryn iOS app. Muryn is designed to provide layered ambient audio for Rest and Focus without a Muryn account or a Muryn-operated backend.

Core audio, artwork, timers, and saved experiences work locally after installation. Muryn does not sell personal information, show advertising, or track you across apps or websites.

The short version: we do not receive your Rest or Focus activity. The only network service built into Muryn is the purchase system described below.

02 · Local information

What Muryn stores on your device.

Muryn uses Apple-provided local storage so the app can remember your choices. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • favorites, saved custom mixes, selected sound layers, and volume settings;
  • a short recently played history and the last environment you played;
  • Focus routines, explicit session outcomes, optional "worked for me" feedback, and adjustments you choose to accept;
  • timer and fade preferences, onboarding state, and other app settings.

This information is stored in Muryn's app container and is not transmitted to PRIZM. Muryn does not use it to create an advertising profile, calculate a health score, or infer a medical or cognitive condition.

03 · Purchases

How Muryn Pro is verified.

Muryn uses Apple's StoreKit and RevenueCat to offer and verify the optional Muryn Pro lifetime purchase. Apple processes your payment, Apple Account, billing information, and refund requests under Apple's own terms and privacy practices. PRIZM never receives your payment-card details.

RevenueCat receives an automatically generated anonymous app user ID, purchase and receipt information, and limited technical information required to operate its service. This is used for app functionality—including purchase validation, fraud prevention, entitlement delivery, and restoration—and aggregate purchase analytics. Muryn does not send RevenueCat your favorites, mixes, playback history, Focus outcomes, contact details, Health data, or advertising identifier.

Automatic device-identifier collection and RevenueCat diagnostics are disabled in Muryn. RevenueCat's handling and retention of information is described in its Privacy Policy. Apple's handling of App Store transactions is described in App Store & Privacy.

04 · Permissions and analytics

What Muryn does not ask for.

Muryn does not request access to your location, microphone, photos, contacts, Health data, or notifications. It has no social feed, user-upload service, advertising SDK, or cross-app tracking code.

PRIZM does not operate behavioral analytics or crash-reporting services inside Muryn. Apple may independently process App Store, device, or diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies; that processing is controlled by Apple, not Muryn.

05 · Retention and deletion

You control local data.

Local Muryn information remains on your device until you delete it. In Muryn, open Settings and choose Delete local Muryn data to clear locally stored routines, outcomes, favorites, mixes, history, onboarding state, and preferences. Deleting the app also removes its app container according to iOS behavior.

Deleting local data does not cancel or erase a purchase held by Apple. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records according to their own legal obligations and retention policies. You can use Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

06 · Sharing and transfers

No sale of personal information.

PRIZM does not sell or rent personal information. Limited purchase information is processed by Apple and RevenueCat for the purposes stated in this policy. Those providers may process information in countries other than your own and apply safeguards described in their respective policies.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal process. Because PRIZM does not receive your Muryn activity or account information, we generally do not possess that information to disclose.

07 · Children

No profile to build.

Muryn is not directed at collecting information from children and does not knowingly collect contact, location, Health, or other directly identifying information from any user. The limited purchase processing described above also applies when a purchase is made through an Apple Account managed by a parent or guardian.

08 · Security

Protected by the iOS app boundary.

Local information is kept inside Muryn's iOS app container and benefits from platform protections such as sandboxing and device data protection. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed absolutely secure, but Muryn minimizes risk by not operating an account database or uploading your Rest and Focus activity.

09 · Your choices

Access, deletion, and questions.

You can review and change your Muryn information directly in the app, clear all local Muryn data in Settings, or remove the app. For Apple transaction or refund information, use Apple's account and support tools. For a RevenueCat privacy request relating to Muryn, contact us with enough context to locate the relevant anonymous purchase record if available; never send payment-card details or passwords.

Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you additional rights. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot lawfully be limited.

10 · Changes

If the app changes, this page changes first.

We may update this policy to reflect changes to Muryn, service providers, or legal requirements. We will post the revised policy at this URL and change the effective date above. If a change materially affects how the app handles information, we will provide additional notice where required before the changed behavior is released.
