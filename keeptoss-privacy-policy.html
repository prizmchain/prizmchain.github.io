Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

KeepToss processes your photos and videos on your device. We do not upload your photo library, extracted text, or cleanup decisions to our servers. No account, advertising, or cross-app tracking is used.

1. Who We Are

KeepToss ("the App") is provided by PRIZM_KR ("we," "us," or "our"), an independent developer based in the Republic of Korea. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information Handled by the App

Information How It Is Used Where It Is Processed
Photos and videos you allow KeepToss to access Display media, identify review groups, compare similar items, estimate blur, and prepare user-confirmed cleanup actions On your device
Photo and video metadata Organize items by date, media type, file size, screenshot status, and related library attributes On your device
Visual analysis results Support duplicate, similarity, quality, and best-shot suggestions using Apple frameworks On your device
Text extracted from screenshots Let you save useful text in the App's local text vault On your device
Cleanup decisions and App settings Resume sessions, remember reviewed items, show progress, and maintain local preferences On your device
Purchase history and entitlement status Unlock and restore the KeepToss Lifetime Unlock purchase Apple and RevenueCat

3. Photo Library Access

KeepToss requests Photo Library permission only to provide photo cleanup features. Depending on the access you grant, the App may read selected or full-library media, create compressed copies, save a still image from a Live Photo, or request deletion of items you have chosen.

  • KeepToss does not delete an item merely because it was suggested.
  • You review the pending list before deletion.
  • iOS presents the final system confirmation for library deletion.
  • You can change or revoke Photo Library permission at any time in iOS Settings.

4. On-Device Processing

Photo similarity, visual quality signals, text recognition, thumbnail generation, and related analysis use Apple system frameworks and KeepToss code on your iPhone. KeepToss does not use a cloud AI service or upload your media for these features.

5. Information Sent to Third Parties

Apple

Apple processes App Store purchases, refunds, and system-level Photo Library actions under Apple's terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your payment card details.

RevenueCat

KeepToss uses RevenueCat to retrieve products, verify the Lifetime Unlock entitlement, and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives purchase and entitlement information and may assign an anonymous App user identifier. We do not provide RevenueCat with your photos, videos, extracted text, name, email address, or advertising identifier.

Learn more in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your photo or video files
  • Extracted screenshot text
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • Precise location
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Cross-app tracking or behavioral advertising data
  • Third-party analytics profiles

7. Local Storage and Retention

KeepToss stores session progress, local analysis caches, reviewed item identifiers, saved text, cleanup activity, free-use count, and preferences on your device. You can remove this local App data by deleting the App, subject to normal iOS and backup behavior. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records according to their own legal and operational requirements.

8. Security

We minimize transmitted data and rely on iOS security controls for local storage and Photo Library access. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but KeepToss is designed to avoid transmitting the contents of your photo library.

9. Children

KeepToss is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require an account or profile.

10. Your Choices

  • Limit KeepToss to selected photos or grant full Photo Library access.
  • Change Photo Library permission in iOS Settings.
  • Choose whether to purchase Lifetime Unlock.
  • Delete locally saved text and cleanup items within the App where available.
  • Delete the App to remove its locally stored App data, subject to iOS behavior.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App, applicable law, or third-party services change. The "Last updated" date will identify the current version.
