KeepToss Support

Help with photo access, cleanup, compression, and Lifetime Unlock.

Contact PRIZM_KR

Email prizm.dev.kr@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, KeepToss version, and the steps that caused the issue. Do not attach private photos unless they are necessary and you are comfortable sharing them.

Photo access

Open iOS Settings, find KeepToss, and review its Photos permission. Limited Access lets you choose specific items; Full Access is required to review the entire library.

Deletion

KeepToss does not delete media automatically. Items enter a pending list, and iOS shows the final system confirmation. Deleted items may appear in Apple's Recently Deleted album, subject to iOS behavior.

Compression

Compression support depends on the media format, iCloud availability, free device storage, and iOS. Keep the original until you have verified the newly created copy.

Restore Lifetime Unlock

  • Use Restore Purchase on the KeepToss purchase screen.
  • Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.
  • Confirm that App Store purchases are available on the device.
  • If restoration still fails, contact us with the error message shown by the App.
