Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED / AUGUST 28, 2026

Plain-language summary. DEEP RUN does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, or a Prizm-operated server. Career progress, tournament state, saved hands, opponent memory, and settings stay in the App's local storage on your device. Prizm does not sell personal information. The current release does not collect personal data from the App.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Prizm ("Prizm," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the iPhone game DEEP RUN: Poker Career (the "App").

The App is a premium, single-player, offline Texas Hold'em tournament career. It simulates poker tournaments using virtual chips, buy-ins, bankrolls, prizes, backing arrangements, and tournament entries. The App does not offer real-money gambling or allow virtual value to be purchased, transferred, withdrawn, or redeemed for money or anything of value.

2. Data Handled by the App

2.1 Data stored locally on your device

CategoryExamplesStorage
Career progress Season and week, virtual bankroll, reputation, event access, tickets, tournament registrations and results, recovery choices, backing state, milestones, and campaign progress. Device only
Active tournament state Virtual stacks and blinds, entrants and table assignments, tournament phase, shuffled-hand seed, public actions, and checkpoints needed to resume a tournament or hand. Device only
Saved hand records Selected hand histories, public action sequences, boards and shown cards, decision review grades, estimated-value results, and variety fingerprints. The App caps the main saved-hand archive at 120 records. Device only
Opponent memory Computer-controlled opponents' public observations, table-image reads, and rivalry history used to make future encounters feel persistent. Device only
Preferences and local identifiers Audio and gameplay settings, migration state, and a randomly generated installation seed used to create repeatable saved tournaments and fair card shuffles. Device only

This information is stored in the App's local iOS container, including iOS preferences storage. It is not sent to a Prizm account, analytics service, or cloud-save server.

2.2 Data the current release does not collect

  • No Prizm account, player profile, name, email address, phone number, or password is required.
  • The App does not include third-party advertising, analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • The App does not request access to precise location, contacts, camera, photos, microphone, Bluetooth, HealthKit, or payment-card information.
  • The App does not provide online multiplayer, Prizm-operated chat, public profiles, or user-generated content uploads.
  • The App does not use Apple Game Center or a Prizm-operated leaderboard in the current release.

3. How Local Data Is Used

  • Save and resume: restore your career, current tournament, table state, and an active hand after the App closes.
  • Career continuity: maintain virtual bankroll, results, event access, reputation, milestones, recovery state, and long-term goals.
  • Persistent opponents: let computer-controlled rivals respond to publicly observed actions and shown cards from earlier encounters.
  • Hand review: replay saved hands and present locally calculated ranges, equity, pot odds, expected value, and decision feedback.
  • Preferences: remember audio and other App settings on the device.

Local game data is not used to advertise to you, build a cross-app profile, determine your identity, or make decisions about you outside the App.

4. Offline Play, Analytics, and Tracking

Core gameplay is designed to work offline. The current release does not transmit gameplay, hand histories, purchase behavior, device identifiers, or diagnostic events to Prizm.

The App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track activity across apps or websites owned by other companies. It does not display advertisements.

Current-release boundary. If a future release adds an online service, third-party SDK, optional content purchase system, account, cloud save, analytics, or another materially different data practice, we will update this Policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before that release is made available.

5. Purchases and Virtual Gameplay Value

Apple processes the paid App download through the App Store. Prizm does not receive your full payment-card number or Apple Account password. Apple may retain transaction, storefront, refund, tax, fraud-prevention, and account information under its own policies.

The current release does not sell virtual chips, bankroll, tournament entries, loot boxes, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. All chips, bankroll values, buy-ins, prizes, backing amounts, and tournament entries shown in the App are fictional gameplay values. They have no real-world monetary value and cannot be deposited, transferred, withdrawn, sold, or redeemed.

Review Apple's Privacy Policy for information about Apple's processing.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Local game data: remains on the device until it is replaced, reset through an available App action, or removed by uninstalling the App, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior.
  • Saved hands: the main archive retains up to 120 records; older records are removed as newer records are saved.
  • Apple purchase records: are retained and managed by Apple under its policies and applicable legal obligations.

Deleting the App generally removes its local container from the device. Data may reappear if iOS restores an earlier device backup. Because Prizm does not receive or maintain a server-side player profile for the current release, Prizm generally has no remote player record to locate or delete.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

  • Start a new career or use available reset and history-management actions, understanding that local progress may be permanently replaced.
  • Delete the App to remove its local data from the device, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior.
  • Manage App Store purchase history and Apple Account privacy choices through Apple's settings and services.
  • Contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, or portability rights that may apply in your jurisdiction.
  • Contact your local data-protection authority if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

Some rights are subject to legal exceptions and our ability to verify and locate the relevant information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

8. Children's Privacy

The App simulates poker tournaments and is not specifically directed to children under 13. It does not offer real-money gambling. A child should use the App only with the authorization and supervision of a parent or legal guardian where required.

We do not knowingly ask children to submit names, contact information, account profiles, precise location, or user-generated content to Prizm. If you believe information from a child has been processed contrary to applicable law, please contact us.

9. Security and International Transfers

Local game data is protected by the security features of your device and iOS application sandbox. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Prizm does not transfer App gameplay data internationally because the current release does not send that data to Prizm. Apple may process App Store transaction and account information in other countries under its contractual safeguards, privacy policy, and applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, integrated services, or legal requirements change. We will revise the date at the top of this page and, when required, provide additional notice or request consent. Materially different data practices will not be applied retroactively without an appropriate legal basis.
