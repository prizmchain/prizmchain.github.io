Duo Prism support

Duo Prism is a single-player optical puzzle game. Arrange mirrors and prisms, then adjust the fold angle to connect virtual light to a target. The game includes 32 campaign puzzles and 8 design challenge puzzles, with hints and touch fold controls.

Need help?
Email prizm.dev.kr@gmail.com. Include your device model, OS version, app version, puzzle or challenge name, input mode and the steps that caused the problem. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending them. Never send passwords, Apple Account identifiers or payment-card details.

How do I play?

Place and rotate the available optical components, choose Observe when the puzzle offers an editing phase, then adjust the fold angle until the light reaches the target. Hints and touch fold assistance are available if you need help.

Do I need an iPhone Duo?

The game is optimized for iPhone Duo. On a supported device and OS with native fold input, folding the device changes the game angle. On other supported devices, the Fold slider provides touch control with the same optical rules. You can also select touch input in Settings under Input and Accessibility. Check the App Store listing for current OS and device compatibility. Move a folding device gently within its normal range; do not force the hinge.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No Duo Prism account is required. Campaign puzzles, design challenges and hints work offline. Optional Game Center rankings and achievements use Apple services and require an internet connection, Game Center sign-in and service availability. They do not unlock paid gameplay content.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress, layouts, local records, laboratory designs and preferences are stored on your device. Duo Prism does not provide account-based cloud synchronization or developer-hosted recovery. Deleting the app or changing devices can lose local progress. Apple device backups may include app data according to your system settings, but recovery is not guaranteed. Removing local progress does not remove records already held by Apple Game Center.

Is there a subscription or an in-app purchase?

No. Duo Prism is a paid download. One purchase includes the current game, its 32 campaign puzzles, 8 design challenge puzzles and hints. The current version has no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. The App Store displays the applicable price in your region.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple’s refund request service. Eligibility depends on Apple’s policies and applicable consumer law.
