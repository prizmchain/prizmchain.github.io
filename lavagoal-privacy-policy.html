LavaGoal — Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 14, 2026

This Policy explains how Prizm (“we”, “us”) handles information in LavaGoal (라바골), our car soccer game, and when you contact us or visit these policy pages.

1. Information kept on your device

LavaGoal does not require an app account. Language preferences, court-panel choices, landing-assist settings and win records are saved locally on your device. The game does not upload those records to a developer-operated server, and we do not provide a cloud-save account.

The app has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking identifiers or developer-operated automatic crash-reporting service. We do not sell personal information, use gameplay information for targeted advertising, or track you across other apps and websites. The game does not request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone or camera.

2. Local multiplayer and network permission

If you choose local multiplayer, LavaGoal asks for local network access to discover or host a nearby game using Bonjour. A randomly generated room label and the network endpoint needed to connect are visible to devices on the local network. The two players' devices exchange gameplay information such as controls, car and panel selections, match state, scores, time, rematch choices and replay events.

This exchange is necessary to run the match. It goes between the participating devices, not through a developer-operated matchmaking or gameplay server. Discovery information may be visible to others on the same network. The current connection uses local TCP without app-level TLS encryption, so play on a trusted network with people you know. The app provides no chat, social feed or publicly uploaded player content.

You can leave the room to end a session or disable local network access in iOS Settings. AI play, practice and local settings do not require that permission. Temporary match and connection state is kept in memory for play and cleared or replaced as sessions end, reset or the app closes; any saved win count and preferences remain on the device.

3. Apple services and device backups

Apple handles the paid App Store download, payment, purchase history, eligible family purchase sharing and refunds under its own terms and Privacy Policy. We do not receive payment-card details through LavaGoal. Apple may separately process store analytics or device diagnostics according to your Apple settings and its policies.

Apple device backups may include local app data depending on your settings. We do not control those backups. Manage backup retention and deletion through Apple's device and account controls.

4. Support inquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, investigate a reported issue and meet applicable legal obligations. Do not send sensitive information or another person's personal information unless necessary and authorized.

Support correspondence is handled through our email provider and retained only for as long as needed to address the request, maintain necessary support records or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us; information that must lawfully be retained will be restricted to that purpose. We do not use support messages for advertising.

5. Visiting our website

These policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitor IP addresses and technical request information for hosting and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages add no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, external fonts or contact forms. Loading a policy page is separate from playing the game.

6. Children and age ratings

The app does not ask for a birth date, require a child account or deliberately collect children's personal information through gameplay. It has no in-app chat, advertising or in-app purchases. Follow the age rating displayed in your region. Parents or guardians can manage purchases and device permissions through Apple's controls. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can address the request and delete information where appropriate.

7. Your choices, retention and requests

You can change settings in the app, end a multiplayer session, revoke local network permission or uninstall the app. Deleting the app removes its on-device data through iOS; retained backups or restored data are governed by your Apple settings. Because we do not hold a gameplay account or server copy of your local records, we cannot retrieve or remotely delete them for you.

For personal information you have sent to support, contact us to request access, correction, deletion or other rights available under applicable law. We may need enough information to verify your request. We do not require you to send identification documents unless legally necessary.

8. Security and updates

We minimize the information the app handles, but no device or network is completely secure. Use supported software and trusted networks. If our information practices change, we will update this page and its date and provide any notice or consent required before introducing materially different processing.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Prizm at prizm_kr@hotmail.com.
