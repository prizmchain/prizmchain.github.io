1. Using TakeAlign

TakeAlign by Prizm helps align a video with a separately recorded audio file, preview the timing and save a new video. Your App Store license is governed by Apple’s Standard EULA; this page explains the product’s usage and purchase conditions without replacing mandatory consumer protections.

2. Supported files and alignment

File compatibility is checked in the app. Automatic alignment needs recognizable shared sound in both files. Manual timing adjustment is available. TakeAlign adjusts a fixed time difference, not gradual recording drift. Results depend on the source material; check timing and sound before relying on a result. The app does not record through the microphone or camera.

3. Free save and Lifetime purchase

One full-length, watermark-free result can be saved externally for free. Making an internal preview or completed video alone does not consume that allowance; a verified successful save to Photos or Files does. Saving the same previously authorized result again does not consume another allowance. A one-time Lifetime in-app purchase unlocks further supported exports. It is not a subscription and does not remove file compatibility or device limits. Prices and applicable taxes are displayed by the App Store before confirmation. Apple handles payment and refund requests. Restore purchases using the Apple Account used for purchase; restoration does not recover deleted media. Deleting projects or clearing cache does not reset the app-wide free-save record.

You retain your rights in your material. Only import, edit and share content you are entitled to use, with any legally required consent. The app does not grant rights to third-party content. You choose the files, alignment, output and sharing destination. Do not use TakeAlign unlawfully or infringe others’ rights.

5. Storage and availability

Keep backups of important original files and saved results. Deleting a completed video or project removes the selected app-managed data and cannot be undone by restoring purchases. Externally saved copies remain under your control. Storage limits, file incompatibility, device failure or app removal can prevent processing or cause local data loss. No particular result or uninterrupted availability is guaranteed; statutory rights and obligations remain unaffected.
