See weekends, seasons and time with people in one calm view. Choose your assumptions, personalize your time portfolio and keep what matters on a widget.

KEYWORDS
life,planner,weekends,seasons,family,moments,milestones,timeline,chapters,widgets,calendar,offline

DESCRIPTION
See the time that matters.

Yours in Time turns a horizon you choose into a personal time portfolio: weekends, summers, shared moments and life chapters. Not a prediction of how long anyone will live. A clearer way to see the time you want to make room for.

Make it yours
• View time in everyday units, from Saturday mornings to Christmases.
• Create custom time assets with your own names, frequencies, icons and colors.
• Edit your assumptions whenever your plans change.

Time with people
Add someone important, name the moments you share and choose a frequency and horizon. Compare what meeting more often could mean over the same period. These are estimates based on your inputs, not lifespan forecasts.

Life chapters
Place past and future chapters on one timeline, from university and early career to plans for retirement.

Keep it in view
Choose people, chapters or time assets for your widgets. Search for an item, preview your selection and change it when your priorities shift. Supported Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets keep meaningful time close.

Private by design
Core planning works offline. No app account, ads or AI. Entries are stored on your device; purchases are handled by Apple.

Free use includes up to 3 people, 3 custom time assets and 8 chapters. An optional one-time Your Time Unlimited purchase removes these item limits. No subscription.
