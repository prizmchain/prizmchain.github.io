Privacy Policy — English

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Who we are

EchoTake is a music-practice app provided by Prizm. This policy covers the app and this policy page. Contact: prizm_kr@hotmail.com.

2. Practice data stays local

Imported audio and video, microphone recordings, lesson names, saved phrases, playback settings, waveforms and preferences are processed and stored on your device. EchoTake has no account system or developer-operated media server. We do not automatically upload your practice content, sell personal data, display ads or include third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. Apple may separately provide diagnostics according to your device sharing settings.

3. Microphone and file access

Microphone permission is requested when you enable recording. Recording happens only during an explicitly started foreground session, with a visible indicator, and stops on interruption, inactivity or screen lock. Denying access leaves non-recording modes available. The system Photos picker provides only selected media; the app does not require broad photo-library or camera access. Files providers may download a cloud-only file that you choose.

4. Purchases and network exceptions

Apple processes purchases and restoration through StoreKit. The app verifies transaction information and stores a purchase-status cache on the device; it does not receive payment-card details. EchoTake does not use RevenueCat. Apple, chosen cloud-file providers and destinations you select for exports process information under their own policies. Apple privacy information: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ .

5. Retention, deletion and backup

Delete lessons and takes using app controls. Unkept temporary recordings are automatically pruned, with limits of five completed temporary takes and 200 MB, subject to active-file protection. Explicitly kept takes and imported originals are excluded from that cleanup. App removal normally removes the local app container, but exported copies, device backups and cloud-provider copies may remain. Manage those with the relevant provider. A manual library backup includes originals, phrases, settings and kept takes; it excludes temporary takes, rebuildable caches and purchase entitlements. Purchase restoration does not restore media files. Operating-system device backups may also include app data under your settings.

6. Optional support and diagnostics

Diagnostics are exported only when you request them. The report contains settings, entitlement state and storage totals, without lesson filenames, titles or media. If you email support, we receive your address, message and anything you attach. We use that information to handle the request and retain it as needed for support or legal obligations. Request access, correction or deletion by email; applicable legal rights remain available. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete practice files stored only on your device.

7. This website and international services

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, including IP addresses, under https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . This page adds no analytics, advertising trackers or external fonts. Apple, GitHub, email and selected cloud providers may process information in other countries under their policies. This differs from the app’s local practice workflow.

8. Children and your choices

EchoTake is designed for music learners, primarily adults. We do not knowingly request children’s personal information or provide public profiles or in-app social sharing. Children should use recording, purchases and external sharing with appropriate guardian involvement. You can revoke microphone access in system settings, use non-recording modes and choose what to export. Contact us about information submitted by a child.

9. Changes and contact

We will publish policy changes here and update the date. Where applicable law requires additional notice or consent, we will provide it. For privacy questions or rights requests, email prizm_kr@hotmail.com.
