How do I change a planning assumption?

Open Settings to change your date of birth or planning age. Edit individual people to change their meeting label, annual frequency, or chosen horizon. Time assets and chapters can also be edited in their respective tabs. Values are based on your assumptions, not lifespan predictions.

How do I choose what a widget shows?

In the app, open Settings and choose what to show on the Portfolio widget. Switch between Time assets, People, and Chapters, or search by name. Preview the item, then tap Save. Cancel leaves the saved selection unchanged.

Add a widget through the iOS widget gallery. To give separate widgets different items, choose Your Time Focus and use the widget's Edit Widget controls. Available layouts depend on the Home Screen or Lock Screen and the widget type.

Why has my widget not updated immediately?

iOS controls widget refresh timing. Open the app to confirm your saved selection and values, then allow iOS time to refresh the widget. Check whether the widget is a Portfolio widget or a separately configured Focus widget. If it continues to show the wrong item, try removing and adding that widget again. Removing a widget does not delete your app entries.

How do I restore an Unlimited purchase?

Use Restore Purchases on the Unlimited screen while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. Internet access may be required. A restored purchase unlocks eligible features; it does not recover local people, assets, or chapters. If a purchase cannot be found or a product is unavailable, contact us before purchasing again.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles payment and refund requests. Follow Apple's refund instructions. Prizm cannot access your payment-card details or issue an App Store refund directly.

Can you recover deleted entries?

Prizm does not receive or keep a server copy of your planning entries, so we cannot recover them remotely. Resetting app data or deleting the app can remove local content. Apple device backups may restore data depending on your backup settings; restoring a purchase is separate from restoring entries.

Does the app predict how long someone will live?

No. Counts of shared moments are approximations calculated from the frequency and horizon you choose. They are not medical, health, or longevity forecasts. You can change those assumptions at any time.
