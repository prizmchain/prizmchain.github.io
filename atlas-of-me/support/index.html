Start with the essentials below. If your atlas still does not look right, write to a human.

Email support
01 · PLACES

Add or correct a place

Open Atlas and use the add control to search countries or cities. Open a visit to change its dates, source, rating, or lived status. Suggested places are never added without confirmation.

02 · PHOTOS

Import from Photos

Choose Import from Photos and grant the access level you prefer. Scanning and grouping occur on device. If a result is wrong, dismiss it or choose the correct city before adding it. You can change photo access in iOS Settings.

03 · INSIGHTS

Why an insight changed

Insights are recalculated from confirmed visits, dates, metadata, ratings, and home city. Adding or correcting those records can change the result. A smaller history uses more cautious wording and may leave deeper sections locked until evidence grows.

04 · NEXT

Understand a recommendation

Open a destination and choose Why this place? to see the gap, preference, novelty, and geographic expansion signals behind it. Use Interested, Not for me, Been there, or Save to improve later results.

05 · PURCHASES

Restore premium access

Open Settings or the premium screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restoration does not create a new charge. Subscription management and refunds are handled by Apple.

06 · DATA

Export or delete your atlas

Open Settings and choose Export Data for JSON or CSV, or Delete All Data to remove local atlas records. Deleting local data does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase purchase history held by Apple.
