Overview

Atlas of Me is a local-first personal travel reflection app operated by PRIZM. It does not require an account, include advertising, sell personal data, or track activity across other companies’ apps and websites. Core atlas records and calculations remain on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the countries and cities you add, visit dates, lived-place periods, ratings, favorites, notes, home city, confirmed photo suggestions, insight snapshots, saved recommendations, preferences, purchase access state, and export settings locally. Exact photo coordinates are used transiently to suggest a city and are not retained as a continuous location history.

You can export your atlas as JSON or CSV and use Delete All Data in Settings. Removing the app also removes local app data according to iOS behavior.

Photos

Photo access is requested only after you choose Import from Photos. Atlas of Me reads creation dates and location metadata on device to group possible places. Photos, thumbnails, asset identifiers, and exact photo coordinates are not uploaded to PRIZM. A suggestion becomes a visit only after you confirm it. You can limit or revoke access in iOS Settings.

Location

If you choose automatic home-city detection, the app requests When In Use location for that action. Manual home-city entry remains available. The app does not request Always location, continuously track your position, or build a background GPS trail.

Apple services and purchases

Map display, optional place lookup, and reverse geocoding may use Apple services under Apple’s privacy terms. Optional premium purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. PRIZM does not receive payment-card details. Apple may provide the app with a transaction status so purchased access can be granted or restored.

Data collection and sharing

Atlas of Me does not send your visit history, notes, favorites, photo library, or precise coordinates to PRIZM. The current release contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, account backend, or cross-app tracking code. User-initiated exports and share cards leave the app only through the destination you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect contact information or directly identifying information from children or other users.

Changes and contact

If data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed behavior is released. Questions or deletion and export support requests can be sent to prizm_kr@hotmail.com.
